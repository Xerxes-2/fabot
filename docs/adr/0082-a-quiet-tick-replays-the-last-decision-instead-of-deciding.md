# A quiet tick replays the last decision instead of deciding

> **Status:** accepted

> **Amended 2026-10-07 (ADR 0091 W5 S5)**: a forced tick's fight-only colonies (ADR 0091's W4b) are the world fight's: their fighters are matched in the one world match beside every deciding colony's, their economy replays its plan off its own carry, and one a fighter's step hands back is decided whole off that match, never by deciding the tick's fight a second time. Decision 4's forcing rules are unchanged; their split into fight and economy facts is W7's.

> **Amended 2026-10-06 (the cadence is tunable)**: decision 1's cadence is `k` ticks per full decide, **default 2** — today's full-then-light — set by `Tuning.Cadence`. A live override, `Memory.fabot.observe.cadence` (`observe cadence <2..4> | clear`), takes its place on the next tick, clamped to 2..4; unset, a bucket line raises it (`Tuning.CadenceBuckets`, shipped as the Lean amendment's half bucket → 3, now falling back only `CadenceBand` = 1,000 over the line). A step plan holds up to four tiles and a decision lays each light tick's own planned acts (`Decision.Later`: a refiller's further pours, a hauler's act on arriving at a later tile), so a plan replays `k − 1` ticks; a forced tick's fresh plan counts its light ticks from its own tick. The forcing rules (4) are unchanged at every `k`.

> **Accepted 2026-09-30**, the user taking every recommendation: a tick of latency outside combat, the `rangedRange` + 2 radius, and a one-to-one cadence. Out of the CPU round that took the mean tick from 86 to about 68 ms by exact cuts alone. The remaining cost is spread over dozens of sites; this is the one structural cut left that is worth double digits. The user chose it over rewriting the projection's collections (B) and caching the snapshot's static structures (C).

Every tick today builds the World (every visible room swept), projects every colony, and runs the whole decision — plan, pool, spawns, the Matcher, the mover — for a fleet that is doing what it did last tick. Measured live 2026-09-30 (t841,9xx, 20 consecutive ticks, 65 creeps): 63 of 65 creeps **kept** their Task, and 13-16 of 65 **moved** on any tick — the haulers (61% of samples), the rangers (26%), and a worker or an anchor now and then; upgraders, reservers and the garrison essentially never. The Matcher is already incremental (a holder is gated against its own Task alone), so the cost is not re-matching; it is re-deriving, every tick, a colony whose derivation has not changed.

We propose that **ticks alternate between a full tick and a light tick, for the whole shard at once**. A full tick is today's tick unchanged, plus one addition: each creep's **step plan** — the tiles of its path from where it stands toward where its Task has it going, as far as its own room's edge — is laid off the flood the mover already ran, and kept on the heap. A light tick builds no World, projects no colony and runs no decision. It re-issues the last full tick's repeatable intents and advances each walking creep one step along its plan, and it checks the few facts that would make that wrong; if any fails, the light tick hands over to a full one on the spot.

## Decisions

1. **The cadence is global and fixed**: a light tick follows each full tick, never two light ticks in a row. Not per colony: the World sweep and the snapshot (~14 ms) are shard-wide and only a shard-wide light tick skips them. Not adaptive to the bucket: ADR 0041 holds — CPU is measured, not budgeted — and a cadence is a schedule, not a budget. Amended 2026-10-06 (live t979,9xx, bucket emptied by ~158 ms cadence full ticks during a war): in Lean (bucket < 5000) a second light tick may follow the first — one full in three — as an emergency stop-gap; the forcing rules still apply, and #549 (per-colony decide on forced ticks) and the CPU round are the real cure. Amended 2026-10-06 (#549; live, forced ticks were a third of all ticks, ~145 ms against ~124 unforced, ~75 ms of it six colonies' decide): a forced tick builds the World anyway, so the reason per-colony was rejected does not hold there. A forced full tick decides only the colonies its facts are theirs (`LightTick.deciders`: a hostile's or a controller's room's colonies and the holders of our bodies standing there, a creep's holder — hurt, also its room's colonies — a fighting structure's colony, the deferred, and any colony with no plan; a fact no colony owns is everyone's), and every other colony replays its last plan exactly as a light tick does (`LightTick.replay`), issues nothing else, and keeps its Memory leaves and its replan turn. Such a tick counts toward the cadence as a light one, so the full tick after it decides every colony and no plan is replayed staler than a light tick replays one (a tick, two in Lean). Its CPU row says how many facts forced it and how many colonies replayed (`facts`, `replayed`), and `observe cpu` reads such a partial tick apart from a whole full one. Accepted: in a room a deciding and a replayed colony share, the deciding colony's mover does not see the replayed bodies' steps, so two may meet on a tile and one loses a tick — the light tick's own cost (3).

2. **What a light tick re-issues**: every act the last full tick issued or planned whose actor and target still stand in the glance, wherever the body stands now — the engine checks range, store and cooldown, and a refused intent costs no CPU. Amended 2026-10-05 (#529), from a list of special cases (repeatable work from the same tile, #498's standing draw, #520's loose-room shots, #528's aims): ObserveRoom's vision landed on the light tick, which reads nothing; a hauler lost a tick a leg; a level-0 core was swung half the time.
   - **an act on an object** — `HarvestSource`, `UpgradeController`, `ReserveController`, `AttackController`, `BuildSite`, `RepairStructure`, `DismantleStructure`, `TransferEnergyToStructure`, `WithdrawFromStore` naming no amount, `PickupPile`, `AttackStructure`, `ClaimReactor` — while `Game.getObjectById` still answers its id (`Glance.Objects`, looked up on a light tick alone); **a shot or heal** while its hostile is in the glance or its creep of ours lives; a **drop** only from the tile it was decided on, its one target being the ground; an **observer's look**, so its vision lands on the next full tick; a **lab's reaction** (`RunReaction`, amended 2026-10-07 by ADR 0089 S3) while its output lab stands — the engine counts each run off the labs' own stores and refuses one on cooldown, so a replay draws nothing twice, and the full tick asks one tick ahead of the cooldown so the run it held back lands on the light tick;
   - **the planned acts** (`Decision.Next`), each replacing the full tick's act on its channel: a refiller's next pour (2026-10-04), and a hauler's act on arrival — one stepping into its Withdraw's, Pickup's or Refill's Work Area is withheld for reach on the full tick and acts on the light one, the act the Emitter would issue on the tile it steps onto (`Emitter.arrival`). A striking body's aims (#528, `LastFull.Aims`): amended 2026-10-05 (#535), from the head of its targets taken with no reach check, which swung at an out-of-reach head beside another body: its whole kill order, a fighter's and the weapon reflex's alike, from which the light tick picks again off the glance the full tick's way (`Emitter.pickStrike`: the first in reach, else the head, or the mass attack, #537, off the ranger's guns and shields the full tick recorded, `LastFull.Volleys`, with any glanced body within three that is none of its aims refusing it; a mass attack is never replayed as an act, for it hits whoever stands near now). The pick replaces the full tick's own strike, is issued on any channel its replayed acts leave free, and a replayed heal last, on what is left — the full tick's own priority (`IntentPlan.arbitrate`), so a swing beats a pre-heal; a melee pick in reach steps onto its target's tile in place of the step plan's step (`Emitter.glueStep`), as on the full tick;
   - **never**: a withdraw or a send naming an amount (one that landed would be drawn twice — under the terminal's fee reserve, past the courier's marked load), `MoveCreep` (the step plan's, 3), `SayCreep`, `SignController`, `ClaimController` (it takes the room, which forces the next tick full, or is refused for a GCL another try does not change), the planner's `SpawnCreep`, `PlaceConstructionSite` and `PlaceSpawnSite`, `SetRampartPublic`, and a structure's fight (`FireTower`, `HealWithTower`, `RepairWithTower`, `ActivateSafeMode`), after which the tick is full (4).

3. **What a light tick moves**: a creep with a step plan whose head is the tile it stands on issues `MoveCreep` toward the plan's next tile. A creep standing anywhere else (blocked, pushed, fatigued) issues nothing and waits for the next full tick. No arbitration is run: a light tick's moves are the full tick's moves one step on, and two plans that meet on one tile cost one of the two bodies a tick.

4. **What forces a full tick instead** — checked at the top of the light tick, over `Game` directly, before anything is issued. Amended 2026-10-06 (#549), from rules read on presence — an armed body in a room we hold a structure in (#461), any body within reach of ours or of last tick's shooters (#462's scouts, #520's loose rooms and harass rangers, #521's rooted bodies as exceptions), any hit lost, and the Lean stop-gaps of 2026-10-05 that read the last two in home rooms only; a paralysed melee our guard was beating in W18S25 forced tick after tick — to one invariant: **a fact the plans did not account for** (`LightTick.forcing`), each the concern of the colonies decision 1 names:
   - a raider — a live weapon or heal, no keeper, no ally — in a room we own a controller in, every tick it stands there, the plans having seen it or not; only that home's colony decides. A home is never a tick late: a drain that turns into a raze, and #545's razed economy, are read on the tick they happen, and the per-colony split keeps the cost to one colony's decide;
   - a hostile that can strike something of ours that it could not as the plans were made, read per body and per structure, now or after one step: within its weapon's range (a structure beside one that only dismantles or claims), one tile more for a body that is not as good as rooted (`Gait.stationary`, #521). Coming into a shot after a step and coming into the shot itself are two facts, so a ranged body walking 5, 4, 3 tiles in forces at 4 and again at 3. Unless what it reaches is a creep of ours that strikes it; an ally, or a body in a room under our safe mode, reaches nothing. So a scout, a cripple whose weapons are shot off, a defender standing off and a keeper on its rock force nothing; a melee on the cripple it fights and a ranger trading shots with its target force nothing; a body new in reach of a hauler, or of a hauler crossing a room where our ranger fights it, does;
   - a creep of ours that lost hits, unless every body that could have struck it is one a striker of ours aims at and reaches this tick, and its loss is within the healing the plans give it (#533's out-damaged reading); a loss to nothing in sight (a tower, a body gone) forces too. Its holder's colony decides;
   - a structure of ours fought on the last full tick: a tower fired or healed, or safe mode was raised — an act no replay repeats. A creep's own shot or heal is not on this list — amended 2026-09-30, when 40 of 83 live full ticks were forced by a harassing ranger's shot at a target already dead or gone: one dead or gone leaves nothing to decide, and a replay drops the shot;
   - any creep of ours standing on its room's border ring with no ground to step onto: the engine carries a body that ends a tick there into the neighbour, so a creep that crossed on the full tick and stood still on the light one would be carried straight back, and every crossing would bounce for ever. A creep on the ring has always just crossed in, so a light tick steps it straight in, or onto an inward diagonal, where the terrain allows (`LightTick.inward`; amended 2026-09-30, when the ring forced 10 of 71 live full ticks); only one walled on all three is forced;
   - any creep of ours that is new since the last full tick, or gone;
   - a fighter of ours whose unarmed quarry walks off where its plan put it, while the chaser can close on it, or whose chase walk an armed body newly reaches (#619, #626; listed here 2026-10-08, #646). The list is no order: `LightTick.forcing` reports the deferred colonies first and the rest in its own;
   - an assault's squad in contact (user, 2026-10-14): an armed body, no keeper, within `rangedRange` + 3 of a squad cast of ours, i.e. its escorts' engage reach and a step. Replayed, a chase against a kiting body stood every other tick. A body on a rival rampart is exempt, since no hit of ours lands on it (the glance carries those rampart tiles in rooms where a hostile stands). It costs the casting colony a decide every tick while the contact lasts;
   - any controller of ours whose safe mode or level changed;
   - a global reset (the reset tick is full, as #442 left it). Amended 2026-10-03 (#488): full, but not every colony decides on it. A cold decide ran 313-504 ms live of the engine's 500, so the reset tick decides the colonies with a threat in a room they project (an armed non-ally body other than a keeper, or one that can hurt something of ours) and the rest in order up to `Tuning.ResetFirstShare` of them (`LightTick.resetSplit`); the others issue nothing that tick, their creeps stand and their Memory leaves keep last tick's values;
   - colonies the reset tick left undecided (`LastFull.Deferred`): the tick after it is full and decides them (and, since #549, only them).

   A new threat is never light: every body that comes to reach something of ours is decided on the tick it does; a fight the plans already hold is replayed — its shots and heals at targets still standing (2) — until the cadence decides it again.

5. **What a light tick writes**: the CPU line (tagged light, so `observe cpu` can split the two populations) and the positions leaf, so `CreepInfo.Moved` keeps meaning "moved since the last tick" on the full tick after it — and nothing else. The Transition log, the Raid logs, the layout and quota leaves, and the assignment table are the full tick's; a light tick holds no Verdict to fold.

## Consequences

- **Expected**: a light tick costs its intents (the engine's 0.2 CPU per accepted intent, ~11 ms of today's ~105 intents) plus the checks in 4 and the step plans, a few ms; the full tick costs what it costs today plus laying the plans. At ~15 ms light and ~70 ms full, the mean falls to ~42 ms, under ADR 0041's revisit line for the first time since it was written. The **peak does not fall**: a full tick is as expensive as today's.
- **Latency**: every judgement is at most one tick older. A replayed Harvest does not look at the store: a miner that filled on the full tick and stands on no container drops one tick's energy beside it, for a hauler to pick up. A hauler that arrives on a light tick transfers a tick later; a spawn idle on a light tick casts a tick later; a body whose store fills on a light tick starts back a tick later. A threat at home, or a step from striking something of ours, is never a tick late, by 4 (#549).
- **Pinned by tests at the Core seam**: the light-tick planner is a pure function of the last full tick's intents and step plans and this tick's creep positions and the 4 facts, so the rules in 2-4 are unit tests; the full tick is unchanged and every existing test holds.
- **Harness**: `npm run profile` runs both kinds of tick; its `decide by colony` table reads full ticks only, and a new line reads the light ticks' mean.
- **Reversible**: one flag in `Tuning` turns every tick full again.

## Considered options

- **Per colony, alternating by colony index** (#357's shape) — rejected: it smooths the peak but skips no World sweep, so it saves the colonies' decide and not the snapshot; ~47 ms against ~42.
- **Skip only colonies in which nothing moves** — rejected on the measurement above: the haulers move on 61% of ticks, so a colony is almost never still.
- **Re-issue last tick's `MoveCreep` directions** instead of a step plan — rejected: a direction is right for one tile only; the next tile's direction is the plan's.
- **Longer cadences (one full tick in three)** — deferred: the latency in Consequences doubles and the saving on the mean is ~10 ms more; a `Tuning` number once two-tick cadence has run live.
- **Rewrite the projection's collections (B), cache the snapshot's statics (C)** — the other two cuts; B is larger and exact, C is smaller. Neither is ruled out by this one.
