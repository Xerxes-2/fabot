## Decision

**A tile is unsafe to one of our bodies when some hostile can bring a weapon to bear on it no later than the body can be on it and gone. Both sides are timed by fatigue and distance alone.**

- **Hostile map.** Per room, per hostile class (strike range × gait): one multi-source flood seeded with each body's fatigue and costed `Gait.ticks` per tile. That cost is exact, because the engine carries no fractional fatigue. Hostiles are assumed to use roads. The result, dilated by the strike range, is `A(t)`. A 0-MOVE body never arrives. Parts are read by #531's window. Against a fighter, a class counts only where it out-damages that body's heal (#533's reading); against a non-fighter, every armed class counts.
- **Our map.** `T_b(t)` by live gait (`rangerGaitOf` generalised) and current fatigue. Built only for bodies where `A` is within the horizon.
- **Borders.** Floods carry through `Atlas.seams` onto landing tiles. An unseen neighbour that had hostiles at its last look (world danger log) seeds its landings as risky.
- **Readers.** Walk avoids `A ≤ T_b + held`. Flee steps to the nearest tile with `T_b < A`. Kite picks a tile within 3 of the target with `A_melee > T_b + fatigue`, one the target cannot leave range 3 from before our next shot. Stand (idle, parked, working, shelter, camp, core ring, line) needs `A > H`, off the exit band and doorways, within the work's range. Glue picks the tile that keeps contact after the target's best step. The light tick forces a full tick when a glanced hostile beats the margin recorded at the planned tile.
- **Pursuit is the mirror.** The evader's region is the tiles it reaches first and keeps range on. The pursuer shrinks it and intercepts the exit or waypoint, not the evader's tile. For melee this manufactures contact. **On evidence:** progress (region, gap), a no-progress budget, a catch rate per owner. This replaces `Gait.outruns`.
- **Horizon** `H` is the ticks to the body's next decision: 1, 2 with a light tick, 3 in Lean (ADR 0082).

**Where it lives.** Hostile maps are world facts. `WorldView` builds them lazily, once per contested room (a room where a hostile can strike, or a `Danger` room), on ADR 0086 S4's per-room Reach. Our floods are per body, kept in the Atlas memo with `Fresh.*` and bounded by live creeps. Quiet rooms pay nothing.

**S1 as built** (`src/Core/Arrival.fs`; no readers, so no profile fingerprint can move).

- **The flood.** One multi-source Dial's bucket flood per class, keyed by the tick a body can step off a tile. A seed is a tile with the tick a body is on it and the tick it can leave: 0 and its fatigue wait (`ceil(fatigue / 2·MOVE)`) for a hostile standing there. A body that leaves at `t` stands on the next tile at `t + 1` and leaves it once that tile's `Gait.ticks` are paid. So a 1:1 body stands on a swamp tile one tick after its step and leaves it five ticks later; the arrival is not the departure. The exception is a step from inside onto the ring: the engine charges it no fatigue (`movement.js`), so the body may leave that tile on the tick it arrives. `A` is the arrival array dilated by the strike range (separable Chebyshev). A 0-MOVE body seeds its tile and never spreads.
- **Classes** are (strike range, pace), the pace being whole ticks for road, plain and swamp. Parts are read in #531's window (`HostileInfo.effectiveParts`, `HostileInfo.gait`). A hybrid is in both its weapon classes, because S3's kite reads the melee map apart. Every armed creep that is not an ally's counts, keepers and Invaders included; diplomacy is `mayStrike`'s business, about what we strike. Each map names its `Members`, so S2's damage gate (Decided 3) can price them.
- **Ground.** `Atlas.arrivalGroundIn` gives the ground with the border ring laid in. For a hostile it is raw terrain, not masked by the keeper margin. It is paved where we see roads, a road on a wall being a tunnel walked at a road's pace. It is blocked by every obstacle and by each private rampart of ours (a public one is open), but never by a construction site of ours: the engine blocks a site only to its owner, and a hostile walks over it and destroys it. For our bodies it is the walking grid, our sites included. A body that crosses exits arrives on an exit tile and never walks on from it, unless it is a seed: the engine carries a player's body over the border the tick it steps on, so it cannot walk the ring. The first cut let every body walk the ring, and a walled-off room was "reached" round it in 47 ticks. An Invader or a keeper, which no exit carries, does walk the ring, in a flood of its own; the class map takes the sooner of the two.
- **Borders.** A seen neighbour's own crossing hostiles (`crossesExits`: never an Invader or a keeper) are flooded alone (`RoomArrival.Own`, undilated). They land on this room's landing tile on the tick they arrive on the neighbour's exit, and may leave the landing that same tick, because the step onto the exit cost no fatigue and the engine carries the body across within the tick (`global.js`). The first cut seeded the exit's departure instead: a 14A1M beside the exit struck (2,y) across the border at tick 15, not 2. The exit pairs are read off both rooms' hostile grounds (`Seam.pairsAcross`, passable on both sides), not `Atlas.seams`, whose rings carry our keeper margin. A neighbour the projection carries no ground for gets a lower bound instead: its road pace times the Chebyshev tiles to each exit pair, landing and leaving on one tick.
- **Unseen risk** (Decided 2). A neighbour in `WorldView.Danger` that nobody sees this tick seeds its landings at 0 into `ArrivalMaps.Unseen`. That map floods at a tile a tick on every ground and is dilated by 3, because the remembered body's class is not kept. World S6 owns a better reading.
- **Hostile fatigue** is glanced as `RoomFacts.HostileFatigue` and `WorldRoom.HostileFatigue`, a map by id, not as a `HostileInfo` field. The record is built at ~320 sites, mostly arena captures. A body absent from the map reads as rested, the safe direction. The light tick does not glance it yet (S5).
- **Sharing.** `WorldView.ArrivalGround` is laid by `sharingSafeGround` beside the Reach table, and is keyed by room and the content of every ground the floods read: the room's hostile ground and each neighbour's (or none). So a room's floods are built once a tick for every colony whose projection lays those grounds alike, and are the same whichever colony asks first. Every fixture, with `None`, builds alone. Our own floods (`Arrival.ofBody`) are built per call, so nothing outlives the tick.
- **Tests:** `ArrivalTests` covers swamp, a shooter and a hybrid, roads, fatigue, 0-MOVE, our rampart private and public, our construction site, a tunnel, an Invader walking the ring where a player cannot, the border carry (a player crosses, an Invader does not), a 14A1M leaving its landing the tick it lands, a swamp exit and landing, the unseen risk and its end, our body's flood, and the share.
- **Known limit: boosts** (lifted by ADR 0089 S0, which reads a boosted MOVE into `HostileInfo.gait`). A boosted MOVE pays off more than 2 fatigue a tick, but the projection carried no boosts, so `Gait.ticks` read it unboosted. A boosted body arrived sooner than its map said, which is the unsafe direction. This season has no market, so boosts are rare. One ADR 0004 test-message citation was dropped to keep the ceiling.
- **Fingerprints:** all nine runs are identical to main (73b0818e; 2f4582b1 moved no source), every leaf and the reset tick.
- **CPU.** These were measured with a scratch Fable 5.18 → Node 22 benchmark on the bench box, calling `Arrival.inRoom` on real terrain with fresh floods each call, averaged over 2,000 runs. A quiet room costs 0.02 ms (its ground and its neighbours' copied for the key). One class map costs 0.07–0.09 ms: one 17M17A, or one 14A1M. 6 mixed hostiles (2 maps) cost 0.17–0.20 ms. 20 hostiles of 4 gaits (4 maps) cost 0.37 ms. 6 in the room plus 4 next door plus an unseen danger room cost 0.27 ms. **A war tick of the #532 shape** (t972,617: Crunchy047 14A1M with fatigue 15 in W18S25, Wraith403 with fatigue 12 in W17S26, maps read in both and in W17S25 between, 4 maps and 2 neighbour floods through the shared table) costs **0.60–0.63 ms**. That is inside the ≤ +3 ms war budget. It cost 0.50 ms before the review's fixes; the difference is the neighbours' grounds being copied and compared for the share key. The first build read Fable's checked `item`/`setItem`, `splice` and closure-indexed dilation, and cost 0.43 ms a map; the `Grid.at`/`setAt` accessors and a bare `pop` cut it to the figures above. A quiet or light tick pays nothing: nothing calls `Arrival` yet. The only per-tick additions are the fatigue map of a room with hostiles in it and one empty table, unmeasured.

**S2 as built** (Reach, Safe, Flee and walk-round read the maps; amends ADR 0033, 0085 and 0010, each with its banner).

- **Reach.** `Threat.arrivalsOf` lays `Arrival.inRoom` for each projected room an armed hostile or a hostile tower stands in, and for each room beside it that a crossing hostile there could land in within the horizon (no farther from the shared border than that). A room of ours under safe mode gets none. A room's Reach is the tiles a body stands on (the walking grid and the ring, `Atlas.standingTilesWhere`) where some class's ticks are at most `Tuning.ReachHorizon`, less our ramparts. The horizon is two ticks: this full tick and the light one after it. It replaces the tile margin `ReachMargin`, which is gone. The keeper margin is now pin + weapon + horizon, still six. `WorldView.ReachGround` shares a room's Reach by its maps' identity and our ramparts. The Reach no longer holds walls or tiles off the projection. A hostile standing off our ground lays only its dilation there.
- **Gone:** `reachRadius`, `Tuning.ReachMargin`, `reachAcross` (the trespass landing reads `Atlas.seams` directly), and the stationary margin in the Reach. `Gait.stationary` stays for its other readers: catchability (S6), light-tick forcing (S5) and the stand-down.
- **Towers (#566).** `WorldView.hostileTowers` reads, off the census of every owner's structures, the towers not ours that fire: an Invader's beside a stronghold core, or a player's in the room that player owns, never an ally's. A tower left in a room its owner lost fires at nothing. Its energy is not carried, so it reads as loaded. `WorldRoom.Towers` carries them, and `Arrival.mapsOf` makes them one class (`Arrival.towerClass`: the room's side for a strike, no legs, ticks 0 everywhere).
- **Damage gate (Decided 3).** `Arrival.strikersOf` prices each class member: a creep at its live weapon of that class (`HostileInfo.effectivePower`), a tower at its falloff from the tile. `Arrival.damageAt` sums the members whose class strikes the tile within the given ticks and that stand near enough: strike + ticks, plus one across a border. It overcounts where one class is many bodies, the direction a body runs in. Flee's out-damage (`Emitter.outDamaged`) reads it at the horizon.
- **Flee.** Flee is applicable where the body's own tile is in the Reach, or, for a body its fatigue holds, where a map strikes the tile within the horizon plus its wait (`Threats.standsStruck`). `Threats.fleeAreaFor` (cached a body a tick) takes one pass over the grid for the safe tiles within a horizon's ticks of the nearest, by the body's own arrival: what a walk to the safe set ends on, as a band so one held tile at the Reach's edge parks nobody, without building the 2,000-tile set. **Not built: "Flee steps to the nearest tile with `T_b < A`."** Narrowing the ground to the tiles the body reaches before any hostile cost the spar bench: `outpost-ranged-healer` never cleared (was t233), kills 3 → 2, cast 3000 → 4500, hurt 4378 → 6050, and `outpost-melee-healer` cleared t92 → t114. Bisected, then confirmed by switching it alone. In a room with no safe tile, the room's towers covering it, Flee's ground is beside the landings across the nearest open border by the body's own walk to its exits, into a room a route enters (`Atlas.routes`: never a rival's, a stronghold's or one withheld from passage). Its price and step cross toward that room (`Pool.travelCostOf`, `stepToward`).
- **Walk-round.** A runner, and a fighter walking to a Guard or an Assault of another room than the one it stands in (`passers`, #533), first walks round the Reach and the tiles a hostile strikes before it could be on them and gone. For a fighter, the Reach counts only tiles where what strikes them out-damages its heal. `Threats.unsafeFor` is a per-index test, never a set. Its body flood is laid only for a body some class can strike by the decision after next with its own lag counted: twice the horizon, plus its fatigue wait, plus its slowest step (a swamp tile held long). Where no walk keeps out of both, the body walks round the Reach alone, and where none keeps out of that, it waits. A runner's walk to work in another room crosses toward that room round them (`Atlas.stepTowardRoomWhere`), since its Work Area is empty from across a border. This is #527's fix: hauler-970335 had stood still between flees for want of a way round.
- **Whole ticks.** An armed body's ranking walk (`FatigueFactor.Whole`: a weapon or HEAL) is priced at two units a whole tick (`Grid.pricingOf`), so a road is no cheaper where it is no quicker. Ties fall to the flood's order. The safer-tile tie-break is not built: the Atlas has no Reach, and S3's kite and glue are where safety enters a fighter's step.
- **Heal reflex.** A fighter owing nothing closes a pressing wound before it casts a shield (`healReflex`): one beside it, at the shield's rate, or a fighter's within three owing at least the healer's whole heal. Then come the shield, then any wound in reach. Under a room's towers the Reach covers every tile, and the siege's medics shielded the whole fighter beside them while the wounded one two tiles off bled. `arena siege defence` scenarios 4, 5 and 8c broke on that until the order changed. A civilian's scratch never outranks the shield (MatcherDecideTests "a medic shields the fighter beside it …").

## Decided (user, 2026-10-07)

1. **Ties are unsafe.** A hostile that arrives on the tick we leave makes the tile unsafe: the W17S26 ranger walked into melee reach on exactly that edge.
2. **An unseen neighbour stays risky until the next look.** Its landings are risky from the danger log's last sighting until vision (an observer or a body) sees the room again; no fixed N.
3. **The damage gate splits by body.** For a fighter, a class counts only where it out-damages the body's heal. For a non-fighter, every armed class counts, because a hauler or miner loses on any hit.
4. **#551's collision keeps `Left` plus a surcharge.** Tiles with `A ≤ 1` cost extra rather than being blocked outright, so a narrow corridor never walls us in.
5. **The budget is ≤ +3 ms per war tick.** The per-owner catch rate lives in the Memory danger log (`observe.world.danger`), so it survives a global reset; it gets a wire-check case.
