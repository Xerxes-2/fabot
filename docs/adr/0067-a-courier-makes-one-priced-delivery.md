# A courier makes one priced delivery

The W15S28 courier carries one 999-unit Thorium load from Storage to the declared sector Reactor in each 636-tick delivery slot (#319). The old 800-tick proposal treated the measured route's 151 movement steps plus three room transitions as 154 elapsed ticks; the production Atlas prices the loaded `[20 Carry; 10 Move]` body at 318 ticks. A 636-tick slot leaves 363 ticks in the Reactor's 999-unit buffer, buying that whole outward leg plus 45 ticks of disruption margin. Although the route leaves enough lifetime for a return, continuity does not depend on the same body surviving another crossing of W15S26's Source Keeper terrain.

The delivery reuses the ordinary priced `Withdraw(Storage, Thorium)` and `Refill(Reactor, Thorium)` Tasks rather than introducing a `Deliver` Task. One fixed `[20 Carry; 10 Move]` body holds the load below the Reactor's 1,000-unit contact cliff, is counted as economically expiring after its one slot, and is charged at 1,500 energy per 636 ticks on the same 1,500-tick amortization horizon as the colony's other rows. A route lock remains rejected by ADR 0066: live danger interrupts the crossing through [[flee]], and the next slot replaces a courier that detoured, died or failed to finish.
