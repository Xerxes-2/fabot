# The refill cluster's capacity is a budget the holders' loads are counted against, and a starved cluster draws the stock

> **Accepted 2026-09-18** on #374, the decision the user's ("同意，就这样做" on options A + C). Implemented by the change that accepted it.

ADR 0054 made the spawn and its extensions one Task and bounded it at `ceil(free / one hauler load)` bodies — *"as many bodies as the ring's free energy divides into loads, so a second one joins only while what stands empty exceeds what the first is carrying"*. The second half of that sentence is what the rule meant; the first half is what it computed, and the two come apart the moment the holder is not carrying a hauler's load. At W15S28 the hauler unit carries 1,500 and the whole cluster is 2,300, so the Task admitted two bodies only under 35% full and one the rest of the time — and *which* one was whoever got there first. Live at t549,000 a worker carrying 530 from across the room held the slot, a courier that had been matched to the ring yielded its tile three times to other bodies, and the courier beside the Storage with 718 aboard was released `capacity-full` and went to refill the controller buffer. The ring sat at 1,214 of 2,000, the spawn at 6 of 300 and not casting, with 234,360 energy in the Storage one tile from the ring.

ADR 0023 put the Storage's energy draw one tier under every container's, unconditionally — stock, not flow — so that a body beside the stock never cycles energy in and out of the same store and the income standing in containers is emptied first. That is right while the colony is casting. It stopped being right in the loop W15S28 was in: rows are sized to the bank's capacity and cast the tick the bank holds their cost (ADR 0021), the hauler row costs 2,250 of a 2,300 cluster, the cluster was filled one body at a time by the rule above, so the hauler gap stayed at one, the flow stayed thin, and the flow was what ADR 0023 was waiting on to fill the ring. The stock was the one thing that could break the loop and the tier gap kept every empty carrier walking past it to a container 75–100 ticks out.

## Decision

1. **The cluster Refill's capacity is a budget, not a count.** `Capacity` gains `Budget: int option`; the Matcher admits a candidate while the energy the Task's holders already carry — the same arrival-counted holders every cap reads — falls short of it. The cluster sets its free energy as the budget and no `Everyone` cap. A hauler carrying 1,500 into a thousand of room admits nobody behind it; a worker carrying fifty admits the courier behind it. Still a number about the Task (ADR 0052 decision 6): the Matcher reads holders' loads against it exactly as it reads their count against a cap, and no Task kind is named.

2. **A starved cluster draws the stock at the flow's rank.** *Starved*: the ring has room and the bank cannot afford the hauler unit it would cast at its own capacity — the body ADR 0050's supply floor casts, read here as an affordability where that ADR reads "can anything refill"; the gate is #374's wording and not ADR 0050's fact. While starved, the Storage's energy Withdraw ranks `Feeding` beside the containers' rather than `StockDraw` under them, so travel cost and not rank decides between the stock and a container; and its capacity is the loads the **ring** can take (`ceil(free / one hauler load)`, at least one) rather than the loads the stock divides into. Everywhere else ADR 0023 stands unchanged: the draw is still pooled only while some other sink is hungry, the Storage's own Refill stays the deepest tier, and the tier gap that closes the cycle is untouched.

## Consequences

- **At RCL4–6 "starved" is the steady state, not an exception.** The hauler unit is sized to the bank's capacity in whole blocks: 1,200 of a 1,300 bank, 1,800 of 1,800, 2,250 of 2,300. So the condition holds whenever the ring is not full (RCL5) or is more than fifty or a hundred short (RCL6, RCL4), and a colony with a stocked Storage tops its ring up from the stock as a matter of course at those levels. Accepted: the cap below bounds it to one or two bodies, the flow is still hauled by every other carrier, the hungry-sink gate still closes the draw the tick the ring is full, and the Storage's own Refill stays the deepest tier so a load that finds the ring full is banked again rather than lost. What is given up is ADR 0023's "flow first" *while the spawn cannot buy a hauler*, which was the loop. At RCL7–8 the same sizing (2,400 of 5,600; 2,400 of 12,900) makes the condition the exception the name suggests.
- The cap is the loads the **ring** can take, not one body: `ceil(room / one hauler load)`, at least one — one or two bodies at RCL4–6, and more at RCL8 where the ring is five times the load. Still #367's lesson read down the energy column — a lift to Feeding without a cap is every carrier draining the stock — and still bounded by what the ring can absorb rather than by what the stock holds.
- The `capacity-full` release ADR 0054 priced still fires when the ring's room falls under what its holders carry, and #230's order still picks the body furthest from the ring. It fires less: room falls by loads poured, and a load poured is a holder that now counts nothing.
- A ring that is nearly full admits a light body and refuses a full one only when the light body's load already covers the room, which is the right answer and the opposite of the old one.
- `Capacity.isBounded` is true for a budgeted Task, so the Matcher walks the assignment map for the cluster as it did under the count.
- Tests: `PoolSeatTests` § refill cluster pins the budget pairwise and the live shape (a light holder and a full joiner); `PoolStorageTests` § storage draw pins the starved tie, its travel-cost resolution, the fed control, and the cap.
