# A Work-heavy body is pinned to its source: the empty window's reprieve is the digging range, the dispatch is a light body's rule, and its Upgrade is in place or nowhere

Live observation of the colony at t155,79x, read off the read-only API, caught three Anchors and two sources arranged the worst way round. W12S28's Anchor was standing in W13S28 at `36,22`, walking back from a room it should never have entered, while its own source at `9,44` held 3,000 energy and nobody dug it. W13S28's own Anchor stood on the Post at `18,3` a few ticks from death, over a source drained until 155,796. And a third Anchor, 1,370 ticks old, was at `33,14` walking toward that same drained source. One full rock with nobody on it; one dry rock drawing two.

Three rules that are each right on their own compose into that. ADR 0025 dispatches a drained source's Harvest to any creep whose **walk covers the wait** — written for a body that pays a tick a tile, and applied to one that pays four to seven (ADR 0006), whose walk therefore covers every wait there is; so the far Anchor is dispatched and the near one, stepped one tile off its container by a hauler drawing it, is no longer the garrison ADR 0024 named and is released `TooEarly`. ADR 0026 discounts an expiring holder at arrival, so the Post a dying Anchor stands on reads free — to the successor it was meant for and to every released heavy body in the colony alike, including one a border away (ADR 0042 put them in one pool, ADR 0041 gave them one price). And the released body's only remaining candidate is Upgrade, which ADR 0016 opened to it at any distance and accepted as one commute; but there is no *once* — every release puts the same body back at the same gate, so it was a commute per release, fifty energy each way against four to eleven idle Work.

We decided **a Work-heavy body is pinned to its source**, in four clauses that read the same one fact — that this body's value is standing still (ADR 0006, ADR 0020, ADR 0021) — and each of which reads part arithmetic and geometry, never a row name:

- **The empty-window reprieve is the source's digging range.** ADR 0025 wrote the full-store reprieve and the empty-window reprieve as one judgement about one tile, ADR 0024's container. They are different questions. Overflow is a fact about the container underfoot, and the full-store reprieve stays exactly where it was. Being in position to dig is a fact about the range, and the engine's dig reaches one tile — so a Work-heavy body **within range 1 of the source** keeps Harvest through the window, container or no container. A hauler bumping the Anchor onto the Seat beside it no longer costs the colony its Post.
- **The dispatch is a light body's rule.** For a Work-heavy body a drained source is early whatever its walk, unless that reprieve holds. Waiting out fifty ticks beside its own rock costs the colony one restock; spending them walking costs the restock, the walk, and the walk home.
- **Its Upgrade is in place or nowhere.** Upgrade is applicable to a Work-heavy body only where it may already act on it — inside the Work Area, this tick, the [[reach]] taken out (ADR 0033). The Dual Seat and the buffer-side row are exactly the shapes that leaves standing (ADR 0020, ADR 0046); what the gate refuses is the walk and never the Task.
- **And so its Harvest survives a full store while it is still walking.** Closing that last walk closed the last way a Work-heavy body could spend anything: ADR 0016 shut Withdraw and the Transfer half with it, ADR 0046 shut Refill, Build and Repair, and this ADR shuts the controller. Such a body never empties — a garrison digs twelve a tick into a fifty store and the overflow falls into the container, so **full is its ordinary condition** — and ADR 0024's full-store gate, read as "this body is done here", then took Harvest away from the very body whose job it is the moment a hauler bumped it a tile. It held no Task, and a creep with no Task takes no step, so it stood full beside a stocked rock for the rest of its life: #193's own symptom, remade by the cure. So the full-store gate is not widened by a tile but by a question. ADR 0024 asks whether a body may keep *digging* where it stands; a body that is still walking is not digging. `mayAct` over the tiles the Emitter acts from is that question — false while the walk is ahead of it, so a full Work-heavy body keeps Harvest and travel cost walks it home; true the tick it arrives, where ADR 0024's condition governs exactly as written and the only tile that keeps a full body *digging* is still the container. The walk is offered only where it ends somewhere — a source with a Post — because every Post is a tile the arriving body has something to do on, and a bare Seat is not.

ADR 0025's Dual Seat exclusion survives the first clause intact and for its own stated reason: on a Dual Seat the body *does* have somewhere else worth being, and it is the tile it is already on. A reprieve there would hold the Anchor on a Task the Emitter will not let it act on and stand it still for the whole window with a load it could have spent — the option ADR 0025 considered and rejected by name. So the widened reprieve subtracts the Dual Seat, and ADR 0024's container condition is not subtracted with it.

## Considered Options

- **Give the Anchor an owning Post — a posting, a home source, a room** — rejected for the fourth time (ADR 0006, ADR 0012, ADR 0021, ADR 0024): it is role binding under another name, it is per-creep state across ticks, and the per-tick rebuild would have to be fought on every front. Pinning is what [[travel cost]] and the applicability gates do between them, and this ADR is those gates finally saying what the row is for.
- **Widen the reprieve to the source's Posts rather than to its digging range** — rejected: the tile the Anchor is bumped onto is not a Post, and that is the whole bug. Range 1 is what the engine asks of a dig, so it is what "in position" means; a Post is what the body competes for, which is a different question (ADR 0024) and is already answered by the Post cap.
- **Widen it further, to range 1 of any Seat** — rejected: two tiles out the body must take a step before it can dig, so it has a walk, and a walk is exactly what ADR 0025 judges. Drawing the line at the action's own range keeps one number in the rule; a second tile of slack is a number nobody can derive, which is the reason ADR 0025 refused a slack allowance.
- **Price the dispatch instead of prohibiting it — let travel cost keep a heavy body home** — rejected for ADR 0016's reason, and it is the same reason a third time: the Tasks compete at the feeding tier, where rank is shared and cost is all that separates them, and a drained Post an Anchor is *standing on* prices at zero for the Anchor beside it too. Cost cannot separate a body from work that is underfoot.
- **Leave Upgrade open to a heavy body and let the arrival gate hold it** — rejected: the arrival gate judges a restock, and a controller has none. Nothing else in the pipeline puts a distance between a full Anchor and a controller fifty tiles away.
- **Cap the walk instead: make Upgrade inapplicable past N tiles** — rejected as the same slack allowance under another name. Work-Area membership is a number the geometry already carries and the Emitter already reads, so admitting exactly the bodies that act this tick costs no new rule.
- **Exempt a freshly cast Anchor so it can leave the spawn during a drained window** — rejected as unearned: it needs a birth test the Matcher has no business making, and it buys at most one restock's wait, once, in a 1,500-tick life.
- **Give the full body its walk home by widening ADR 0024's own condition to the digging range** — rejected, and it is the reason ADR 0024 gave: on a Dual Seat, or on any Post with no container under it, range 1 is a tile the Emitter *will* let a heavy body dig from, so a full body there would keep Harvest, keep the Post, and dig twelve a tick onto the floor (ADR 0012). The store gate has to stay shut wherever the body may act, which is exactly what asking `mayAct` says.
- **Drop the store gate for a Work-heavy body altogether — full is its ordinary condition, so let fullness never end its Harvest** — rejected for the same reason one tile further out: it is the widening above with no boundary at all, and it deletes ADR 0024's sentence rather than reading it. What ADR 0024 decided is that a full body may keep digging only where the overflow is caught; what this ADR adds is that a body which is not digging is not covered by that sentence in the first place.
- **Offer the walk home wherever the source is, Post or no Post** — rejected: with no Post the Work Area falls back to the bare Seats (ADR 0020, ADR 0045), so the walk ends on a tile the body may dig from and may not dig on, and the stall reappears at the far end of a walk. A source with no Post is a light body's rock and its full-store rule is left exactly as ADR 0024 wrote it.

## Consequences

- ADR 0016's Consequence "the one remaining detour — a full Anchor off-post matching Upgrade once — empties it and converges, and is accepted" becomes **never**: a full Anchor off its Post and outside the controller's Work Area is not a candidate for Upgrade at all. What it does instead is walk back — it keeps Harvest for the source whose Post it came off and steps onto it — so ADR 0046's "or holds no Task at all" is reached only where that source has no Post to walk to.
- ADR 0024's full-store reprieve keeps its container condition and gains a second clause beside it, not inside it: a Work-heavy body that may not yet act on the Harvest keeps it. The three ADR 0024 pins that stood a full Anchor off its container now read `Kept` and a step toward the Post instead of `Released none-applicable`, and each keeps its own claim by asserting that no dig is emitted where it stands.
- ADR 0025's Consequence "a fresh Anchor at the spawn is dispatched the tick its walk equals the remaining wait" holds for light bodies only. A fresh Anchor whose target Post is over a drained source waits beside the spawn instead, at most a restock's fifty ticks, once. Accepted.
- The Post a dying Anchor stands on stays reachable by exactly the body ADR 0026 discounted it for — a freshly cast successor, which is walking rather than waiting only because its own source is stocked. Nothing in ADR 0026 changes; what changes is that the pool of released heavy bodies that could race it is now empty.
- `garrisons` splits in two. The full-store widening in `applicable` keeps ADR 0024's container condition and stands beside the walk-home clause above; the empty-window reprieve in `tooEarly` is the new range test less the Dual Seat. The Atlas grows the two room-aware joins they need (`standsAtSource`, `standsOnDualSeat`) beside `catchesOverflow`, so no `Set<Pos>` is compared across a border (ADR 0041).
- A drained source with no heavy body in range still pools Harvest and still dispatches **light** bodies by ADR 0025's rule, unchanged. The Seat cap admits them; the Post cap, which counts heavy bodies alone, is what the returning Anchor gets its tile back through (ADR 0024).
- The transition log gains no reason. The heavy arm reports the same `too-early` pair every rejection does — the walk it would have made against the wait it does not cover for it — so the gate reads as one gate with one reason (#88), and the idle Verdict beside it is `none-in-time` exactly as it was.
- The DecideTests surface: the widened reprieve with its light-body rival and its full-store half, the distant heavy body's refusal and its light-body rival, the two-room case the report was filed on, the Upgrade gate with its in-place half over a pool the source is taken out of, and the walk home driven a tick forward onto the Post. The ADR 0016 pin "an Anchor that is already full off-Post commutes once, then settles" becomes the walk back, ADR 0046's `6W/1C/1M` pin loses its Upgrade, and the three ADR 0024 container pins are rewritten as above. The AtlasTests gain the two new joins, each with the room guard and the range-not-Seat choice pinned.
