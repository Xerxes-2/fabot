# A Layout guarantee that degrades says so on a colony channel, because a footing has no creep

ADR 0022 and ADR 0027 promise one [[link footing]] per target — one beside each planned source [[container]], one beside the controller container, one beside the [[storage]] — and the [[layout]] delivered that promise with a fold that quietly did nothing when a target had no candidate tile left. W12S28 had already fallen through it (#77, found by #65's live verification): the RCL4 placement burst was planned by a bundle that predated #64's reservation and built an extension on `11,43`, the last tile within range 1 of the `10,43` source container. The room holds three footings where the ADRs promise four, and nothing anywhere says so — no [[verdict]], no census, no line. A guarantee that can degrade in silence is not a guarantee, and that is the part worth fixing independently of the room.

We decided **a footing target the Layout cannot serve is recorded on a colony-level observe channel of its own**, the way the [[raid log]] is (ADR 0028), read from the terminal with `observe.mjs layout`.

- **Not a Verdict, for ADR 0028's own structural reason.** Every Verdict case names a creep, the [[transition log]] is keyed by creep and prunes a creep's whole timeline the tick it dies, and the Planner returns no Verdicts at all. A footing has no creep to key one on. That is exactly why the Raid log got a channel rather than a Verdict, and the argument applies here unchanged.
- **Not a console line.** The bot writes no console output anywhere, deliberately. A line fired once on the census-change tick is gone the tick after, and `observe.mjs console` is a live tail rather than a record — an operator asking "does this room still have all its footings" three days later would get nothing.
- **Not a field on the heap.** The [[layout]] and the plan memo are heap state, never written to Memory. A field nobody outside the process can read is not a surface.
- **The record is the current plan's, not a history.** No fold, no ring, no baseline: one leaf holding this tick's unserved targets, each by its tile and its **kind** — source container, controller container, storage, which the fold knows by construction because the target list is assembled from exactly those three. Under a stable census it is the same list every tick, which is what makes it cheap: it rides the plan memo under ADR 0017's standing invitation and is recomputed only when the [[census signature]] moves, exactly like the site Intents and the hauler quota beside it.
- **Written every tick, the empty list included.** ADR 0028's reason, applied to a second record: the leaf's presence is itself the signal that the deployed bundle records this at all, which is what lets the reader tell "no channel" from "nothing lost" instead of answering an operator on a stale deploy with a confident false negative. Three distinct answers, and all three are load-bearing.
- **The search rule does not change.** Range 1, off the trunks, off every other target, off the tiles already taken, nearest the spawn. Widening the search to range 2, or letting a footing displace a *planned* clustered pick that has not been built yet, are revisions to ADR 0022's rule and belong to their own decision. This ADR makes the loss visible; what to do about a loss is the next question, and #100 is the first instance of it.

## Considered Options

- **Leave the fallthrough and accept the loss** — rejected, and it is the whole point: the loss is acceptable, the *silence* is not. Nothing here changes which tiles are reserved.
- **Widen the footing search to range 2** — deferred to its own decision. It changes what a footing *is* (a link transfers at range 1, so a range-2 footing serves nothing), which is ADR 0022's subject and not this one's.
- **Let a footing displace a planned clustered pick** — deferred likewise. It is defensible — a footing already outranks the tower and the extensions, and the reservation is widened by the footing count for exactly that reason (ADR 0027) — but it is a change to the precedence rule, not to its observability, and it would not have saved `11,43`, which was already built.
- **A Verdict for the Planner** — rejected structurally, above. It would also mean inventing a Verdict case that names no creep, which every consumer of the vocabulary would then have to special-case.
- **Fold it into the Raid log's leaf** — rejected: different lifetimes. A raid is episodic history that must survive the tick; a footing shortfall is a property of the current plan and is worth nothing as history, because a plan recomputed from a moved census supersedes it entirely.
- **Record the footings the Layout did place, too** — deferred, and it is the obvious next field. `orchestration.md` notes that anything the bot never emits — a reservation, a footing — can only be cross-checked by a second derivation; a record of the placed footings would turn a #65-style live verification into a comparison. One more field when someone needs it.
- **A per-room record rather than a colony one** — rejected as premature: ADR 0005's single-colony assumption still holds, and the [[layout]] is computed for one room.

## Consequences

- `planLayout` returns its unserved targets beside its Intents, and the `| [] -> taken` fallthrough is gone. The tiles it reserves are byte-for-byte what it reserved before: the fold accumulates a second value and nothing else.
- `PlanMemo` gains `UnservedFootings`. `Decision` already carries the memo, so no seam widens; a recalled plan reports exactly what it reported when it was computed.
- `Memory.fabot.observe` now carries four leaves — `creeps`, `verbose`, `raids`, `layout` — and each writer still assigns only its own.
- `FootingKind` is a closed union with its wire spelling in Core beside it, and `Core.Tests` round-trips it against the union itself the way it does the four Verdict vocabularies (#80's rule): a kind added without a name is a red test, not a silent miss. It is the first non-Verdict vocabulary on that channel.
- `observe.mjs layout` joins the read commands with `--json` like the rest, distinguishing the three answers above.
- The Layout channel has no fold, so it has no `ObserveTests` surface and no `load`. There is nothing to decode and therefore nothing to degrade: a corrupt leaf is overwritten on the next tick.
- The regression test is W12S28's own shape, synthesised: a source whose only Seat is its container's pick, walled on five sides with its source on a sixth, the trunk road out on a seventh and a standing extension on the last. That fixture is what #77 was filed for, and it fails against the old fold.
- The first thing this channel will report on the live room is `10,43`. Whether to demolish `11,43` to reclaim it is #100, and it waits on #84's fill order rather than on this.
