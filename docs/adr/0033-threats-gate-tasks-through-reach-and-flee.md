# A Threat gates Tasks through its Reach, and a creep inside one flees

> **Amended 2026-09-06** (user request after #217): while the home room is under **safe mode**, a hostile standing in it is no Threat — the engine refuses every harmful act there — so it derives no Reach and nobody flees; the colony works beside it. The home room alone: safe mode is the controller's room's, and a raider in an outpost keeps its Reach.

Player giaco's squad probed `W12S28` for 220 ticks from t68824 (#66) and the colony survived it by geometry: the raiders stood at the bottom exit where the single tower does its minimum, and never came within 25 tiles of a creep. The left exit is 19 tiles wide, ten tiles from the tower, and opens onto the working ground — a raider entering there reaches the upgraders in about five ticks — and nothing in the Task pipeline reads `Snapshot.Hostiles`: a creep being shot keeps standing on its Seat until it dies, silently but for the [[raid log]]. ADR 0007 called threat location "a post-RCL3 concern" and ADR 0014 left hostiles out of every gate on purpose; both deferrals have now run out. We decided **hostiles enter the Task pipeline as a colony-level fact, never the spatial projection**: a **Threat** is a hostile with an ATTACK or RANGED_ATTACK part, its **Reach** is the tiles it can hurt, a Task whose [[work area]] lies wholly inside a Reach is inapplicable to everyone, and a creep that can run and stands inside a Reach is matched to a new Task, **Flee**, that walks it out. Movement, pricing and the census-keyed memos stay hostile-blind.

- **A Threat is read off the parts, never the owner.** ATTACK or RANGED_ATTACK; nothing else hurts a creep. A healer, a scout or a claimer is a hostile the [[fire reflex]] shoots and the [[raid log]] records, but it gates nothing; a claimer is the [[safe-mode reflex]]'s business (ADR 0007). An NPC invader and a player's raider do the same damage per part, so the owner is not consulted.
- **Reach is weapon range plus a margin, less our ramparts.** Range 1 for ATTACK, 3 for RANGED_ATTACK, plus a margin of two tiles — one for the hostile's next step, one for our own tick of lag — and the margin is a tunable beside `repairTrigger`, not a term of this decision. A tile under one of our standing ramparts is in no Reach at all: a creep on its own rampart cannot be attacked, and that exemption is what lets an [[anchor]] on a ramparted [[post]] keep digging with a raider beside it (ADR 0034).
- **Reach subtracts from a work area the way occupancy does.** At applicability time a Task's work area is taken less every Reach; empty means inapplicable to every creep, and its holders are released with a reason of their own, `Threatened`, so the [[transition log]] tells a raid's release from a Task that vanished. Non-empty means the mover is handed the remainder — a creep works from the safe half of its area rather than abandoning the Task because one corner is hot. The [[atlas]]'s memoised work areas are not touched; the subtraction is a filter at the point of judgement.
- **Flee is a Task, because movement has one arbiter.** A Flee reflex emitting moves beside the Resolver would be a second source of Move Intents, which ADR 0012 ruled out for the Anchor and the Resolver exists to prevent. As a Task it costs no new concept: applicable to any creep standing inside a Reach, no part or capacity requirement, uncapped; its work area is every walkable tile outside every Reach, so travel cost walks the creep to the nearest safe tile; it sits in a tier of its own, **Safety**, ranked above the [[downgrade deadline]]'s Upgrade, because no other work matters while a creep is being killed. It has no action: the Emitter issues movement only. It ends by its own applicability — a creep outside every Reach no longer qualifies, is released `Inapplicable`, and rematches to ordinary work, whose own areas the same subtraction has already judged. Glyph 🏃.
- **A body that cannot run does not try.** Flee is inapplicable to a [[work-heavy body]]: at four to seven ticks a step an Anchor leaving its Post neither escapes nor digs. It stays and works, its loss regrows under ADR 0012's fleet arithmetic, and the answer for the Post is a rampart, not flight (ADR 0034).
- **The spawn holds while its doorstep is hot.** No spawn Intent is emitted while any tile adjacent to the spawn lies inside a Reach: a creep born into a Reach is a kill delivered.
- **No hysteresis, on purpose.** A squad stepping in and out of the room makes a Reach appear and vanish, so a threatened Task blinks in the pool — the wobble ADR 0025 removed from Harvest. It is accepted here because its cost is idle ticks, not deaths, and because ADR 0028's rule is to measure before designing against it: the pair of `Threatened` releases per approach is visible in the [[transition log]], and a lingering Reach (a Threat's last tile held for some ticks after it leaves) is the named second step if the record asks for it.

## What this overrides

- ADR 0007: "threat *location* is a post-RCL3 concern (towers, ramparts — issue #19's non-goals)". The concern has arrived; the field list it reads — position and body — was already grown by ADR 0014 and 0028, so the projection widens by nothing here.
- ADR 0014: "Hostiles stay out of the spatial projection — they block no tiles, price no paths, gate no tasks." The first two clauses stand and are restated above; the third is reversed. ADR 0028 repeated the same three and its "no fleeing, no Task release, no rank change, no spawn response" is likewise superseded — that ADR said in as many words they were #66's open questions.

## Considered Options

- **Hostiles as spatial obstacles, or Reach as a pricing surcharge** — rejected, as ADR 0014 rejected it: the Atlas's floods and the walk tables ride the [[census signature]] (ADR 0017, 0032) and a hostile moves every tick, so either every memo is invalidated on every tick of a raid or a second invalidation rule is added beside the census. The pipeline already had release and idle vocabularies and a transition log to write them to; the reflex layer's alternative — condition → Intent outside the pipeline — would put a second hand on the movement wheel.
- **Flee toward a named refuge tile** — deferred: it needs refuges that exist (an empty rampart to stand on), and "out of Reach" already answers the question a refuge would. When a room holds bare ramparts, Flee's work area can prefer them.
- **A whole-room gate — no work at all while a raid episode is open** — rejected: it turns a probe at the far corner into 220 ticks of idleness, and it hands the decision to a record ADR 0028 built to be read and never obeyed.
- **A Task threatened if any of its tiles is in Reach** — rejected as too eager (a wide Upgrade area with one hot corner stops all upgrading); **only if all its tiles are** — rejected as too lax (the creep picks the hot tile). Subtraction is the rule that gives both cases the right answer.
- **Fleeing Anchors regardless** — rejected: see above; a crawling Anchor loses the last ticks of digging it had and dies anyway.

## Consequences

- `ReleaseReason` and `RejectReason` gain `Threatened`, with wire spellings beside their unions per #80; a creep on the [[verbose list]] shows the rejection per candidate. `Task` gains `Flee`, `Tier` gains `Safety` at rank −2 beneath the deadline's −1.
- The Threat set, the Reach and the safe-tile set are derived once per tick from `Snapshot.Hostiles` and the rampart census, colony-level and shared by the applicability gate, Flee's work area and the spawn hold.
- Flee's work area is nearly the whole room, and its travel cost is one flood per fleeing creep — the same per-creep flood every placed creep already pays, so a raid tick costs no more floods than a quiet one.
- The [[chat bubble]] table gains 🏃.
- Implemented by #103, sequenced last of the three #66 issues (#101 → #102 → #103), after ADR 0034's ramparts stand on the Posts, so the Work-heavy exemption is a corner case by the time it is live.
