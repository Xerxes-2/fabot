# The refill cluster is one Task

> **Amended by #230** on the release this ADR's Consequences priced and declined to cure, and on nothing it decided: when a cap shrinks, the holder released is the one with the largest `Atlas.walkTicks` — the body furthest from the Task — and no longer the one whose name sorts later. The fix was filed separately for the reason recorded below, that it is the Matcher's release order for **every** capped Task and not this one's rule, and that is where it landed. The bound, the cluster's identity, its Work Area and the Emitter's pick are all untouched, and so is the release's *cadence*: what moves is which of the converging bodies pays, and the one already standing beside a member with a full store now keeps the Task and pours.

One loaded body changed its Refill target every one or two ticks. It was matched to the nearest empty extension; while it walked, somebody else filled that extension; the Task left the pool and released it `task-gone`; it was matched to the next nearest, and so on until its load went into the fourth. Every re-match is a fresh flood, and a cluster with five loaded bodies around it produced several a tick. Measured over t191,307–192,637 on bundle 4e11f3b, **fifty-four of the window's seventy-three `task-gone` releases were Refills** — repair twelve, pickup three, withdraw three, build one — and twenty-seven of the fifty-four were a single hauler. `decide` ran 26 ms of a 34 ms tick for twenty creeps in two colonies, some ten more than the same code cost that morning; the profile harness was level against the parent commit, so the cost was activity and not code.

The mechanism is the Task's **identity**. A Refill was one structure, so a ring of ten extensions was ten Tasks of capacity one apiece, and the first arrival at any of them evaporated everybody else's. Nothing was wrong with the matching: a hungry extension really did stop being hungry.

We decided **the flow's Refill is the [[refill cluster]] — the colony's spawn and every extension of it, as one Task**:

- **The identity is the cluster, keyed at its spawn** (`RefillCluster.ofRefillables`): one Task per colony, pooled under the spawn's id, standing while *any* member has room. Membership is by kind and not by geometry — every spawn-feeding structure of the colony is in the one cluster, however far the Layout put it from the spawn — and the walk stays short because the Work Area below is the union of the members' rings and the mover aims at the nearest of them. Two spawns in a room are one cluster with two doors, keyed at the **lower id**: the key is an identity and never a price, so it is taken by the one ordering that does not move with the [[layout]]. The [[hauler unit]] quota resolves this same sink at the *cheapest door* (ADR 0052 decision 4, "the cluster is one place and not one per spawn"); this key is deliberately not that number and never has to be, because no walk is priced to it. A colony whose Refillables hold no spawn at all keys no cluster and its extensions stay one Task apiece; the live colony has never been that room. A tower is a Refillable and never a member: it is surplus-tier work standing wherever the Layout put it (ADR 0010), not part of a ring one visit tops up.
- **Its [[work area]] is the union of the hungry members' rings** (`Atlas.buildWorkArea` over `actionTilesOf`). Hungry and not every member: a body aimed at the ring stops beside whichever member is nearest, and if the full ones counted it would stop beside one of those and stand there with a full store — this ADR's own defect, re-entered through the geometry.
- **The transfer's target is chosen at arrival, by the [[emitter]]** (`Atlas.refillTarget`): the member with room that the body is actually standing beside. The Planner names a place and the Emitter names the structure, so an extension somebody else topped up mid-walk costs the walker a neighbour rather than its Task.
- **Its [[capacity]] is `ceil(free energy / one hauler load)`** (`Decide.planPool`), a `Total` over every [[body class]]: a second body joins only while what stands empty exceeds what the first is carrying. Divided by the [[hauler unit]]'s cast at the richest bank and never by a candidate's own carry, for the reason every store here is — a capacity is a fact about the Task, and one sink must not answer two numbers depending on who asked (#161). It is a count of **loads and not a rate**: a body pours one member's free capacity a tick — the engine's `transfer` takes no amount and a Refill issues one Intent — so this number says how many stores the ring can take and says nothing about how fast it fills.
- `task-gone` then fires when the **whole ring** is full: once a fill, rather than once an extension.

The tier is untouched: the key is a spawn, so the Refill layering (ADR 0010, ADR 0012, ADR 0023) reaches Feeding through the arm it always did. The [[storage]]'s Refill, the [[buffer]]'s and the [[ferry]]'s sink are unchanged and stay exactly as bounded as they were.

## Considered Options

- **Count a Refill's holders by the energy in flight rather than by the bodies that have arrived** — the ticket's own minimal alternative: `hasCapacity` would read a claimed extension as full, so a second body would pick another. Rejected: it prices *this* creep's load against a store, which makes a capacity a fact about the candidate — the reading #161 and #196 both refused — and it does not touch the failure at all. The churn is not two bodies converging on one extension; it is one body walking to an extension a *third* creep's reflex or an adjacent hauler fills. The Task would still vanish, and the flood would still be paid.
- **Leave ten Tasks and damp the release** — keep a holder on a Task whose target has filled until it reaches the ring. Rejected: it needs a Task that outlives its own condition, which is the shape ADR 0013 took out of Harvest and ADR 0025 took out of the restock gate. The pool is rebuilt from scratch every tick precisely so that "the Task exists exactly while its condition does" is checkable.
- **A Task kind of its own, `RefillCluster`** — rejected on ADR 0010's own grounds: same act, same applicability, same Intent, and every exhaustive match over `Task` would grow an arm that answers what the Refill arm answers. What is new here is the target's *identity*, and the target has been the thing Refill layers by since ADR 0010.
- **Make the Work Area every member's ring, hungry or not** — rejected as the defect in a new place: the ring fills from its outer edge, so the tile a body's walk ends on is beside the members most likely to be full, and it would arrive with a full store and nothing to pour.
- **Key the cluster on the [[layout]]'s checkerboard rather than on the spawn** — rejected: the Layout's picks move (ADR 0040), and an identity that moves is an assignment that is released for a reason no [[verdict]] can name. The spawn is the one member a cluster always has.

## Consequences

- **Free capacity rides on the [[atlas]]** (`Atlas.Cluster`), which is otherwise geometry. It is there for the reason `Tuning` is: the Work Area memo is keyed on the Task alone, and four readers — `workArea`, `mayAct`, the mover and the Emitter — would otherwise each be handed the number and be free to disagree about it. The disagreement's shape is a body standing beside a full extension.
- **This amends ADR 0029's Consequences**, and 0029 carries the banner. Its "the Matcher binds the walk and forces it only in the two gates that spend it… most of the pool is Refills, Withdraws and surplus work that asks for neither" no longer describes the flow's Refill: the cluster is capped, so the capacity gate forces a walk for it. What it costs is bounded the other way — the pool holds *one* of these where it held ten, and the walk is forced once per candidate instead of a flood being paid per re-match. The Storage's, the buffer's and the ferry's Refills are unbounded or bounded exactly as before.
- **A loaded body is matched to the ring once per load poured into it**, and no longer once per extension somebody else filled first. That is the acceptance criterion, and it is what the CPU saving is: the flood a re-match pays for is not paid four times. It is *once per load* and not *never*, and the qualification is the bound's own price — `ceil(free / one load)` only falls, so the tick the ring's free energy crosses a load boundary one of the bodies aimed at it is released `over-capacity`. Which one is the release fold's creep-name order and not proximity, so it can be the body that had already arrived while a walker keeps the Task (pinned in DecideTests, "a load the ring no longer has room for is released capacity-full"). Accepted rather than fixed: the release happens only where the cap is two or more — a ring drained by a large cast, with two bodies converging — and at a cadence twenty to thirty times coarser than the `task-gone` churn it replaces, and the two candidate fixes both cost more than the churn does. Exempting an incumbent from the shrinking cap re-opens the pile-on this bound exists to stop, and releasing the *furthest* holder instead of the first-named is a change to the Matcher's release order for every capped Task, not this one — filed separately.
- The bound is also the first thing that stops the whole loaded fleet converging on one ring. Ten Tasks of capacity one spread the crowd by accident, badly, at the cost of a release per body per tick or two; one unbounded Task would have gathered everybody and left the [[buffer]] and the [[storage]] unvisited.
- A cluster whose members the projection cannot place answers the way every unplaceable thing does (ADR 0004): no tiles, no area, no Task worth walking to — and where `mayAct`'s own permissive escape lets a body act anyway, `refillTarget` names the cluster's own hungry pick rather than nothing. `refillTarget` takes the Refill's structure id and not a whole Task, so the one way it answers `None` is a cluster with nothing left to pour into — which is the postcondition the Emitter reads.
- DecideTests pin the capacity pairwise (a hundred of room admits one body, three hundred admits two), the walker keeping its Task while the near extension fills, the whole ring full releasing it `task-gone`, and the Emitter pouring into whichever neighbour has room. AtlasTests pin the union area, the full member contributing no tile, and the pick — each of the last two as a pair, so it is room and not id order deciding.
