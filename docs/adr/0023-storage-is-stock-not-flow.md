# Storage is stock, not flow: deepest Refill, second-tier Withdraw, pooled only while another sink is hungry

> **Status:** amended by 0071, #216 R5, #232, #505, #530, 0094

> **Amended by ADR 0094** on the second-tier Withdraw: the Storage's energy draw is priced at what the hungriest sink it serves is worth, never more than the flow, and half of it for the re-haul (all of it for a starved ring, ADR 0071's tie). A source's container ranks over it by its overflow pressure. Under the emergency reserve the stock's Refill keeps its rank over the ferry; the buffer is priced by the bodies it feeds, so a buffer the upgrader row's body idles beside is fed first. The deepest Refill and the pooling gate stand.

> **Amended by #530** (live 2026-10-05: W12S28's and W13S28's haulers flipped Withdraw(storage) ↔ Refill(storage) every tick or two, each released `inapplicable`). One haul trip rule, read in four places. (1) **A draw is capped by its sinks' room**: the stock's carriers are admitted `ceil(min(stock, room − aboard) / load)`, where room is every pooled energy sink but the stock's own (the cluster, the towers, the buffer, the ferry's lend, the terminal's energy) and aboard is the energy carried by the holders of those sinks' Refills — supply already committed there, so a convoy rooms off or a courier bound for the Reactor counts for nothing; the reserve and starved arms read the same helper (`Pool.drawLoads`), and a consignor's ore draw is capped by its terminals' room. A Work body draws the stock over a load, as before: its sink is its own spending, which the Upgrade keeps open. The pooling gate (any sink with room) stands. (2) **A load is never poured back into the store it came from**: the considered option "remembering where a load was drawn from" is taken after all, as `CreepInfo.DrawnFrom` (store and tick) off a Memory leaf of its own (`observe.drawn`). It is set by an energy Withdraw the body held last tick while standing beside its store (one still walking there has drawn nothing), replaced by a draw from another store, cleared by any other intake (a Pickup or a Harvest mixes the load) or an empty store, and it **expires `Tuning.DrawnHold` (50) ticks after the draw**: it breaks the immediate flip and must never strand a body whose only sink is that store. `Refill(S, Energy)` is inapplicable to a body holding a live record of S. The first Consequence's accepted churn is therefore delayed by the hold, not abolished. Energy only: a Thorium load held through a full terminal would age its body to death. (3) **A body past half full tops up** from a store within `Tuning.TopUpReach` ticks of walk whose whole holding fits in its free room, before a delivery that can wait — #232's half-empty reading otherwise stands. (4) #232's worth-the-trip line gains a floor (#236): with **no stocked Storage** to fall to, a store at `Tuning.PickupThreshold` is worth the trip. The urgent sinks a top-up yields to, and the Matcher's order, are ADR 0010's and ADR 0002's #530 banners.

> **Amended by #505** (user, 2026-10-04: every Storage stood near 0 while the towers were full). Under the colony's [[emergency reserve]] the Storage's Withdraw opens only for the refill cluster, the towers and a struck or dying rampart's Repair; its carriers are capped at the refillables' room over a hauler load, and a body with a Work part is admitted only to an emergency Repair's slot, or for the cluster when no carrier of the colony is alive — so "Workers with a Work part draw from it on the same terms" no longer holds under the line. The Storage's own Refill rises a rung over the buffer and the [[ferry]]'s sinks there, so income banks first. Accepted cost: a carrier's load can still go to the buffer once the ring fills under it (at most the loads admitted, and the Storage now outranks the buffer for it), and workers under the line harvest or draw containers.

> **Amended by #374** (ADR 0071) with one exception to the second-tier Withdraw: while the colony is **starved** at its refill cluster — room in the ring and a bank that cannot afford the hauler unit it would cast — the Storage's energy draw ranks with the containers' and travel cost decides, capped at the loads the ring can take. At RCL4–6 that condition holds whenever the ring is not full, so at those levels the exception is the steady state of a colony with a stocked Storage; the deepest-tier Refill, the hungry-sink gate and the tier gap that closes the cycle stand as written below.

> **Amended by #216 R5** on where the two orderings live: the Storage's deepest-Refill and second-tier-Withdraw layering is now a `Priority` the Planner sets on the pooled Task, not a tier the Matcher derives (ADR 0052 decision 6). The ordering is unchanged, and the pooling gate keeps its rule and gains a third sink to count: the [[ferry]]'s (#222) — a bootstrapping child's upgrade [[buffer]] is a Refill target that is not the Storage, which is the whole of the condition, and it is the one target the stock is explicitly hired against. The "so the in-and-out cycle has no tick in which both its halves are applicable" clause is still false for the reason #82 gives, and the tier gap is still what actually closes the cycle.

> **Amended by #232** on what reaches the stock at all. A Withdraw is applicable only to a body the store can **half fill** — `stock * 2 >= FreeCapacity`, judged in `Decide.applicable` against the asking body, the mirror of the half-empty rule the same gate already carried. The tier ordering below is unchanged and is still the whole of the ordering; what it could never do by itself is let a hauler *reach* the stock, because a source [[container]]'s Withdraw outranks the stock's for exactly as long as it is applicable, and #161's [[capacity]] admits a drawer to a store holding one energy. Live, W12S28 2026-09-07: a 24C/12M hauler matched a container holding ~200 at t194,906 and was released `inapplicable` at t194,948 — forty-two ticks draining an [[anchor]]'s trickle up to half a load — while the Storage held 263,803 and the spawn stood at twenty-eight. So "haulers empty the sources first and draw on the stock only when the containers are dry" now reads: only when what stands in the containers is not worth the asking body's trip. The stock's **own** Withdraw is outside the line and must stay outside it: what the line buys is the fall to the tier below, and below this one there is nothing to fall to — a gate that refuses here refuses the colony's last intake, and a stock drawn down to four hundred would stand unhauled while the spawn stood empty. Two more stores are outside it: a tombstone or a ruin, which ends on its own, and the [[buffer]] under a [[standing body]]'s own feet, which is no trip at all (ADR 0046, #205). The [[pickup]] keeps the half-empty reading it has and gains no worth-the-trip line of its own (75edfee, #167): a pile decays and a container does not.

RCL4 unlocks the Storage, and the obvious wiring — a Withdraw source and a Refill target like the containers — recreates two failures the colony has already met. A hauler beside a Storage that is both an intake and a sink cycles energy in and out of the same store (the ADR 0019 loop, whose Work-part cure cannot apply: the haulers that must feed the spawn from Storage are the bodies with no Work). And a Storage that stands beside the spawn wins every travel-cost tie against the source containers, so haulers drain the stock into the spawn while the source containers fill and the Anchors' overflow drops and decays. We decided the Storage's two roles are both ordered so that it never outbids the flow: **as a Refill target it is the deepest tier**, below the controller container — surplus reaches it only when every other sink, the upgrade buffer included, is full; **as a Withdraw source it sits one tier below the source containers** — haulers empty the sources first and draw on the stock only when the containers are dry; and **its Withdraw is pooled only while some Refill target other than the Storage has free capacity** (the ADR 0013 shape: the Task exists while the condition holds), so the in-and-out cycle has no tick in which both its halves are applicable. Workers with a Work part draw from it on the same terms; nothing about the Storage is body-specific.

## Considered Options

- **Same feeding tier as the source containers, travel cost deciding** — rejected: it is the leak described above; the source containers are flow and must be emptied first.
- **Container stock as a matching weight** — rejected: rank and travel cost are the matching key's two dimensions (ADR 0002); an amount-aware third dimension is a new class of rule for a problem two tiers already solve.
- **Storage Withdraw for Work bodies only** — rejected: it halves the Storage's purpose, which is to feed the spawn when the sources are dry.
- **Remembering where a load was drawn from** — rejected: assignments are the only thing remembered between ticks.
- **Storage above the controller container in Refill** (stock first, upgrade later) — rejected for now: the colony's goal is RCL, and the controller container feeds it directly; "stock first" is the RCL6+ posture once links move energy for free.

## Consequences

- A hauler that fills from the Storage while the controller container is hungry and finds it full on arrival puts the remainder back into the Storage — a residual churn of at most one load, accepted.
- The Storage is not a repairable kind (it does not decay) and never a trunk hub (ADR 0022).
- The Storage's 30,000-energy Build sits in the surplus tier beside Upgrade, unpaced (ADR 0011), and delays RCL5 by roughly the ticks it takes to bank that much — accepted.
