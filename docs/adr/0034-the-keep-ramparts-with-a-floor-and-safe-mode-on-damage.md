# The Keep: ramparts with a floor, safe mode on damage, and the raid's cost in hits

> **Amended by #201** on the room both of this ADR's readings of `Snapshot.Hostiles` name. The safe-mode arm's "while any hostile stands in the spawn room" is the **[[home room]]** (`Decide.hostilesAtHome`), and the raid log's damage is summed over the ticks a hostile stood there rather than over the episode's whole window. Both for one reason: that ticket widened the sweep to every room the colony works and can see, and the Keep, its ramparts and the controller the stock is spent on all stand in one room. Left unnarrowed, an episode opened by a raider a border away would have recorded this room's rampart decay — three hits a tick apiece, the ride-along this ADR priced as a rounding error against "the hundreds a raid takes" — as the whole cost of a raid that never touched it. The floor, the Keep's full-hits line, the covering rule and the damage measure itself are unchanged.

The same raid (#66) showed the other half of the exposure: the spawn, the tower and — since RCL4 — a Storage holding tens of thousands of energy stand bare, the single tower is the only thing between the west door and all of them, and the [[safe-mode reflex]] spends only on a claimer at the controller (ADR 0007, 0015). ADR 0033 lets the creeps that can run get out of the way; this ADR is for what cannot move. We decided **the colony has a Keep** — the spawn, the tower and the [[storage]], the structures worth defending — and three rules hang off that one list: **a rampart covers every Keep structure and every [[post]]**, kept at a **floor** of hits by the Repair pool; **safe mode fires when a Keep structure is below full hits while a hostile stands in the room**; and **the Keep's hits, and the ramparts', enter the projection**, which is what makes the other two readable and lets the [[raid log]] record damage at last.

- **Ramparts over the Keep and the Posts, and nowhere else.** The Keep because it is what a raid is for; the Posts because a [[work-heavy body]] cannot flee (ADR 0033) and a creep on its own rampart cannot be attacked — the rampart is the Anchor's whole defence, and it makes the Post's tile exempt from every Reach. Not the extensions (cheap, twenty of them, no creep lives on one), not the controller [[container]] (its upgraders run), not the upgrade area. The set is a rule, not an enumeration — a three-source room ramparts three Posts — and it is placed the tick the thing it covers stands, at any level: ramparts are legal from RCL2 and there is nothing to wait for.
- **A rampart's Repair reads a floor, not half of max.** ADR 0010's trigger, below half hits, is the wrong shape for a structure whose max is three million at RCL4 and grows to three hundred: it would be hungry forever. A rampart enters the Repair pool below the **floor** and is whole at it. The floor is a tunable, and the number is derived — the ticks the room must hold times the damage per tick it must hold against; against the squad that came, 180 a tick, 100,000 hits is 555 ticks, two and a half times the raid that was seen. That costs 1,000 energy to raise and, at 300 hits of decay per hundred ticks, one Repair visit per rampart every two hundred ticks to hold — cheap enough that no hysteresis is needed: one visit at 600 hits a tick puts it back over the line. The ADR records the derivation; the number is the code's.
- **The Keep's whole line is full hits.** Spawn, tower and Storage join the repairable kinds with a trigger of their own: below max is hungry. Two reasons: they do not decay, so "below max" means "was damaged" and nothing else; and the safe-mode trigger below reads the same fact, so a Keep left dented would keep the trigger armed for every hostile that wandered through afterwards.
- **Safe mode fires on damage, not on approach.** The second trigger has the first one's shape (ADR 0015 — hold until the harm is certain): any Keep structure below full hits while any hostile stands in the spawn room. Any hostile, not only a Threat: a WORK-only dismantler hurts a structure without ever qualifying as a Threat, and "the Keep is losing hits with someone here" is the honest reading whoever is doing it. Stateless on purpose — the Snapshot is one tick, and comparing against last tick's hits is heap state the colony has so far declined to keep (ADR 0012, 0017). The price of statelessness is the window between a raid leaving and a worker patching the Keep, in which a second squad's mere presence would fire the stock; that window is a few hundred ticks against a stock of three, and a squad arriving on a dented Keep is not a bad moment to spend one.
- **The tower still only shoots.** ADR 0014's exclusion stands, though its arithmetic is corrected in passing: tower repair is 80 hits per energy at range ≤5 against a creep's 100, so 1.25× to 5× dearer across the falloff, not 4–50×. Under fire the creeps that would repair a rampart are inside its attacker's Reach and ADR 0033 holds them off, so a rampart being hit is not being repaired; the floor is sized for that, and safe mode is the answer past it. A tower that repairs in peace, or under fire, is the named next step if a raid ever chews through a floor.
- **The raid log records the cost in hits.** ADR 0028 deferred damage until a decision read hits; now three do. Each episode gains the total hits lost across the Keep and the ramparts inside its window, folded from the previous tick's hits the way losses are folded from the previous tick's names — decreases summed, repairs ignored. The closest-approach measure does not change: ramparts are not "ours" in the sense that number answers.

## What this revises

- ADR 0010: Repair's "below half hits" becomes the trigger of the decaying kinds only; ramparts read the floor, the Keep reads full.
- ADR 0022: "a link footing is the one structure allowed on working ground" — the footing is the one *footprint* allowed there. A rampart is no footprint: walkable, blocking nothing, taking no tile from the Seat it covers.
- ADR 0028: damage in hits is now recorded, and "no ramparts" no longer holds.
- ADR 0007's rule that the field list grows the tick a reader exists is what admits the hits: Repair, the safe-mode trigger and the raid fold are the readers.

## Considered Options

- **Ramparts over the extensions too** — rejected: 3,000 energy each and scattered, no creep stands on one, and twenty more floors to hold is twenty more Repair visits per two hundred ticks for structures a raider has to choose over the Keep.
- **Rampart repair with hysteresis (hungry below the floor, whole at floor plus a band)** — rejected as unnecessary: decay is 3 hits a tick and one repair is 600, so the pool blinks once per two hundred ticks per rampart, which is not a blink.
- **Safe mode on a Threat entering the Keep's Reach** — rejected: the squad that came pokes in and out of the room's edge; a proximity trigger would have spent the stock on a probe, and the stock is three with a fifty-thousand-tick cooldown.
- **Hits compared across ticks for the trigger** — rejected, see above; the stateless reading is right in every case but a dented Keep, and Keep repair closes that.
- **Tower repair of ramparts** — deferred, not rejected: it is a fire-versus-hits trade the tower has not yet been asked to make.
- **Post containers in the safe-mode trigger** — rejected: invaders chew containers as a matter of routine and a container is 125,000 hits; the stock is not for that.

## Consequences

- `StructureKind` gains `Rampart` as a placeable kind; the [[layout]] emits its sites under the covering rule; the [[census signature]] already keys on structure kinds and positions, so a rampart standing is a new signature and a fresh plan, with nothing added to the memo.
- `Spatial.Hits` carries spawn, tower, Storage and rampart hits; `isRepairable` becomes a per-kind whole line rather than a boolean.
- The safe-mode reflex gains a second arm and keeps its gates (stock remaining, not already running).
- `RaidEpisode` gains a damage total and `RaidState` a hits baseline carried while an episode is open, as `Living` is.
- Implemented by #101 (ramparts and Keep, sequenced first of the three #66 issues) and #102 (the safe-mode arm), then ADR 0033's #103 — the ramparts change no creep's behaviour and can stand on the Posts before Flee is live.
- The Storage's rampart is the only thing standing between a raider and the room's largest store of energy; the closest-approach number stays measured to the Refillables and creeps, so the Storage is watched through its hits, not its range.
