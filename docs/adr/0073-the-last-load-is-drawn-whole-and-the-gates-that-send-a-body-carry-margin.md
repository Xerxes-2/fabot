# The last load is drawn whole, ore beside the Reactor is the delivery's, and the gates that send a body carry margin

> **Status:** accepted

> **Accepted 2026-09-18** on #378; the decision is the user's ("做吧", on the four parts as filed). Implemented by the change that accepted it.

Read live at t559,469, after the user found the streak about to break for the third time in a day. The Reactor held 295 T and burned 1 a tick. W15S28's Storage held **376 T**. `hauler-558190`'s tombstone in W15S25 — the Reactor's own room — held **500 T**, decaying. The colony's only courier was hauling energy out of a W15S27 container. Every deposit we own was mined out but W11S29's, which needs RCL6. So the season's whole remaining accessible stock was about a thousand units, none of it moving, with the multiplier at 4 and 295 ticks left on the clock.

Three of the four causes are the same sentence written three times: **the programme is written for a full load.** `Facts.courierProgrammeOpen` opened on `stock >= Tuning.ReactorLoad`, `Planner.deliveryWithdraws` drew on the same number, and `Emitter` used the exact `ReactorLoad` as the marker that tells a drawn delivery from a mine haul. 376 is not 500, so the programme was shut, the draw was not pooled, and the remainder was unreachable by construction — the same shape as #305 one store over. Meanwhile the ore in the Reactor's own room was pooled (#354, #359 reach an errand room) but ranked `StockDraw`, which three crossings from home loses every travel-cost tie to the energy work at the door: #367's finding, one object over.

The fourth is a habit rather than a rule. `Emitter.outlivesTheLoadedLeg` admitted a body on `TicksToLive >= walk * MineContactAgeing` — the bare equality, against a walk the Atlas *prices*. Every difference between that price and the walk a body actually makes came out of a margin that did not exist: a [[flee]] (ADR 0033), a [[keeper margin]] detour, one swamp step, a tick spent yielding. `hauler-558190` drew with about two ticks of slack over a 196-tick leg and died in sight of the Reactor with the load aboard. The user's words, and they are the general point: *"你们写代码习惯一点余量都不留的吗"*.

## Decision

1. **A partial load is drawn when it is the last one.** `Facts.deliveryLoad` answers a whole `Tuning.ReactorLoad` while ore is still coming — a deposit of ours with something left to dig, or ore standing in a mineral [[container]] (`Facts.oreStillComing`) — and the **remainder** once it is not. The programme opens on any stock in that state, the draw is filtered on that amount, and the Withdraw Intent names it. While the mine still feeds the bank the full-load gate is unchanged, so a trickle passing through the Storage never hires a courier for forty units.

2. **What marks a delivery is one fact, and it pools a sink rather than refusing one.** `Facts.carryingADelivery`: the exact `ReactorLoad` (ADR 0067's historical marker), or this tick's own load, or — in a colony that declared the errand, with nothing left to dig and nothing in a mineral container — any ore aboard. The draw empties the bank, so the remainder a body is carrying is measured against a load of 0 on the tick after it took it; without the third reading the Reactor's sink would stop being pooled for exactly the body that is walking it there. What makes the body *prefer* the Reactor is the tier gap that was always going to decide it (ADR 0023): the Reactor's Refill is Feeding and the Storage's is Stock. The Storage stays a sink for the tick the Reactor cannot be one — a full store, a body out of leg — and refusing it outright was tried and taken back out, because `oreStillComing` is false for every mined-out colony and the refusal then reached the last mine haul, a crossed room's sweeper and an arriving consignment's carrier (#349), which is #262's stranded body.

3. **Ore lying in a declared [[errand]] room ranks with the delivery.** A pile or a tombstone beside the Reactor is `Feeding` at a [[capacity]] of one, and `Planner.reactorRefills` opens on it, so the body that fetches it puts it into the Reactor five tiles away rather than carrying it three crossings home. Ore in a merely **crossed** room keeps `StockDraw` and its walk home: out there the nearest store really is the Storage, and banking it rejoins the programme.

4. **The life gate carries slack.** `Tuning.DeliveryLifeMargin`, a percentage over the leg's own arithmetic, defaulting to 25. A gate written as an equality against a priced walk is a gate that fails on any real difference between the price and the walk.

## Consequences

- The remainder of a season's ore is now deliverable, which is what the rule is for: the last 376 T is 1,504 points at the 4 pt/T band and 376 more ticks of the streak that band rests on.
- Decision 4 is a rule about this gate and an argument about every other one. Where a gate compares a body's budget against a *priced* quantity, the price is an estimate and the comparison wants slack. No sweep of the other gates is made here; the knob exists so the next one has something to read.
- ADR 0067's "one 999-unit load" was already amended to 500 by #354; decision 1 amends it again, to "a whole load while there is one, and the remainder when there is not". ADR 0057 decision 4 carries the same banner.
- Decision 3 does not widen what a colony may plan against in an errand room (ADR 0060 decision 1): the kinds admitted out there are still `erranding`'s two decaying ones, and what moves is where they rank and where their load goes.
- No floor is set on the partial load, deliberately: ore is the season's constraint and energy is not (`docs/research/thorium-season-plan.md`), so a courier bought for a small remainder trades 1,500 energy for score that cannot be had any other way. If the row is ever seen hiring against a trickle *while the mine still runs*, the bug is in `oreStillComing` and not in the missing floor.
- Tests: `ErrandTests` pins the last load and its Intent amount against the still-mining control, the remainder's preference for the Reactor **and** its ability to bank, a mined-out colony still banking ore into its Storage, the errand room's ore at the Feeding tier with a capacity of one and the Reactor as its sink, and the margin — a body with exactly the leg's own arithmetic and no slack is refused.
