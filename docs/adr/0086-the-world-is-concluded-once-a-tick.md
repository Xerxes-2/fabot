# The world is concluded once a tick; a colony owns bodies and economy

> **Status:** superseded by 0091

> **Superseded by 0091** on 2026-10-07: its Decision (a colony owns its bodies; the answering colony pools the Task) and its rejected option "a world layer that also matches and casts centrally" give way to 0091's "the world decides the fight once a tick; a colony decides its economy". The code it shaped stays until 0091's W5 replaces it. S1–S5 below are the as-built record and stand; S6 folds into 0091's W1/W2.

> **Proposed 2026-10-05** on an inventory of main c9deda59 and live play at t973,265–973,581; S0 at main feaee027. **Accepted by the user 2026-10-06.** It amends ADR 0047 and 0052 (the `ColonyView` as decide's only input), 0043 (the stand-down as a per-colony log) and 0085 (the Brief keyed per colony); each carries an "amended by 0086" banner. S0's characterisation tests are commented `Characterisation (the world layer's S0)`.

The shell already builds one `World` a tick (`World.ofGame`), and six memories already live on it once: `Sightings`, `Towered`, `ExitWatches`, `Healers`, `ArmedSeen` and `RivalControllers`. The split into colonies starts at three points:

1. **The cut.** `ColonyView.ofWorldRecalling` narrows every room fact, the hostiles included, to one colony's scan set (`World.scanRecalling`).
2. **The memory.** The Raid log is one `RaidState` per colony (`observe.colonies.<home>.raids`). `Observe.standDown` turns it into that colony's `StandDown` gate *before* the cut, so the gate decides which rooms the colony even sees.
3. **The pricing.** `decide` runs once per colony. Each run builds its own `Threats`, Briefs and `Facts.answered` verdicts, and the Raid-log fold prices `Facts.answer` again on the same view.

So the world's facts are read from the engine once, and then narrowed and re-concluded once per colony. Most of 2026-10-05's seam bugs come from that.

## Decision

**The world layer is concluded once a tick and shared by every colony. A colony owns its spawns, its economy and its bodies.**

- **Computed once, keyed by room:**
  - vision: every room seen by any creep, structure or observer of ours, for every reader;
  - each room's hostiles, control and strength;
  - the room's verdict and the core of its Brief (targets, ground, rung, verdict);
  - the danger log: the stand-down rows, rival latches, holds, threat memory and fight records;
  - the raid roster;
  - sightings;
  - the danger maps (Reach, Safe).
- **The watched set** is every room some colony declares, raises, garrisons or answers. It is world-wide, and a shut room stays in it. Shut withholds economy bodies only. It never hides a room from a conclusion.
- **The answerer.** For each room that needs an answer, the world layer picks the colony among all colonies' bodies, ranked as `harassCasters` ranks colonies: by hops, then fitness. Each combat Task is pooled by exactly one colony, so the flat `assignments` leaf never double-writes.
- **A colony's view** becomes its economy plus a read of the world layer. Its `StandDown` is a per-colony *projection* of the world danger log: whether this colony's economy bodies work room R. Matching, casting and quotas stay per colony.
- `Lendable`, `ChildOutposts`, the cross-colony `mayLend`, `Defenders`, `LoadedTowers`, `MinedRooms` and `Seen` fold into world queries and go.

**S1 as built** (on main a6a03387). `WorldView` (`Views.fs`) is built once a tick in `Main` off the `World` and every colony's gate, and every `ColonyView` carries it as `World`: each seen room's hostiles, cores, fallen and control, the watched set, `Danger` (the watched rooms some log holds a raid row, a chased row or a threat memory for), and the tick's shared safe-ground table. `Observe.foldRaids` re-reads its standing raid rows off a view with the world's vision of their rooms laid in (`ColonyView.lookingInto`), and only those rows: the episode roster, losses, cores and reservations stay the colony's own vision's. It prices a raid row on an outpost as the outpost it is and stamps `LastSeen` on any sighting of the raid, whatever the verdict. A Kite then sets the row's `Chased`, which pools the chase through the existing `ChasedOutposts` projection. A look that finds the room empty clears `Chased`, and the out-of-sight chase reads the row alone, so an observer looking every other tick cannot toggle it. `ChildOutposts` is narrowed by the child's chain alone (and a stronghold seen there), not her stand-down, so `scanRecalling`, `roomsProjectedRecalling` and `ofWorldRecalling` lost their `shut` argument. The observer's first tier is `Danger`, each room the nearest observer's alone (`WorldView.lookerOf`) and re-looked once its sighting is 5 ticks old, so the other tiers keep their turns. `Atlas.walkableTilesExceptIn` shares a raided room's safe set between colonies with the same grid and Reach. The arena passes every colony's shut map as `Main` does and has a `Lookout` for an observer it does not hold. The light tick still reads per-colony roles, and S2 picks that up. All nine profile fingerprints are unchanged, `--raided` included: no profile scenario holds a stand-down row. CPU, interleaved over 5 pairs of `siege --raided` with identical fingerprints: mean 14.7 → 13.5 ms a tick, and `walkableTilesExcept`'s inclusive share fell from 14.9% to 10.3%.

**S2 as built** (on main c54fbd2d). `Observe.Danger` is the world danger log: one `DangerRoom` a room (`Down`, the stand-down row; `Hold`; `Threat`; `Watch`, the exit watch) under `observe.world.danger.<room>`, each part written only while it holds and decoded alone, and the rival latches (`Rivals`) beside each rival room's owner under `observe.rooms.rivals`, an entry `{ o?, since, lastLooked }` for a latched room and the bare owner otherwise. Each colony's gate is `standDown` over `Danger.into` (its own log with every world row, hold, threat and latch laid in), so a room shut, impassable, held or threatened is so for every colony. The fold is `Danger.foldColony`, threaded through the deciding colonies in order: a colony folds its own log (`foldRaids`, unchanged) over the world's, less the standing raid rows of rooms it does not price (those it declares, works, or sends a ranger to), so it never re-prices or strikes another colony's raid row with its own bodies; it writes back every row it was handed, and a row it opens in a room whose standing row it was not handed (a core or a reservation it saw there) merges into the world's (`mergeDown`: the later expiry and basis, the OR of the stronghold bit, the earliest opening). Holds, threats and latches are world facts each colony moves only for the rooms it saw, so the order does not matter for them. One row a room: a raid's bounce is read at the opening into `Bounced` (`bounced` on the wire), since the earlier row is gone the tick after. The world ring is trimmed once (`Danger.trim`, `capEpisodes`, never a standing row). **Migration**: until `observe.world.danger` stands, `loadDanger` merges every colony leaf's legacy `outposts`/`holds`/`threatened` (`Danger.ofColonies`: each room's standing rows merged, else its latest; the later hold and threat) and `loadRoomLatches` every `rivalHeld` (`Danger.mergeRivals`: union, earliest `since` and `lastLooked`); `saveRaids` stops writing the four keys, and once the world leaf stands a legacy key reads as absent. The migrating tick writes the room latches whatever they read, since the merged latches equal the loaded ones and the colony leaves' copy is gone. **The deploy is one-way**: there is no dual-write, so a bundle older than S2 rolled back onto this Memory reads no stand-down rows, latches, holds or threats (its colony leaves no longer carry them) and reopens every shut room, a stronghold's included, until its own vision re-concludes them. The heap's exit watches are written into the log every full tick and seeded off it on a cold heap (#542). The double-latch pin flipped: one latch, one stride. Light tick: `LightTick.hurting` is the one reading of who can hurt us this tick (a non-ally, non-safe-moded hostile with something of ours in reach), which the near rule now reads and #549's forcing rules are to stand on; the reset split counts a colony with a declared room the log holds danger in (`Danger.standing`) as threatened, seen or not. **S3 as built** (on main ea7cc424). `Facts.judge` lays one `RoomVerdict` a raided room into every view (`WorldView.Answers`), in `Main` once it knows which colonies decide this tick, and in the arena after its cut. It is priced on first read over every colony's view, and every reader takes it through `Facts.verdictOf`: `answered`/`answer`, `rangersWin`, `raidDeadlines`, `strikeGone`'s chase, `Brief.Verdict` and a chase's capacity (`threatsHeldBy`), `residentsWanted`, `holdsFight` and the musters. A room the world priced nothing for (no raider, or a fixture of one colony) is priced over the reading view alone, by the same function, its own colony its caster. Only the colonies that see the raid price it: a room in their own projection, or one of their own stand-down rows priced out of sight with the world's look laid in (`Observe.Danger.outOfSight`, the standing raid rows among `Facts.pricedRooms`, the one room set the danger log's fold prices too). The pricing runs in two halves. **The room's own answer** (the row's biggest cast, then the biggest ranger kiting it) is its **casters'**. These are the colonies whose rows cast for the room (`castsFor`: their home, declared outposts, ranger rooms, harassment rooms cast, defended children's homes). Where no colony casts for it and it is no home of ours, every colony that sees its raid counts; a home is priced by its own colony's row only where no other colony's row casts for it (a raised child's home by the mother's resident row alone), never by a neighbour's bank. A mother crossing her child's outpost therefore wins and kites nothing there with her bank. **The bodies** are the rangers standing in the room or beside it of every colony that sees it, together, and only those their colony sends: a colony that decides this tick sends any it may lend, one replaying its plan only those already holding the room's Guard. Each is lent by its own colony's rule in its own view (`lendableTo` over `rangerMayKill`), so the lender's quiet is read on the lender's side. A colony that works no room of the raid lends nothing: its rangers next door are no body of the verdict, since nothing of it would send them. The raid's strength (its damage, heal and hits, for the rows, the kite and the bodies alike) is read off the world's vision of the room and the rooms beside it, whichever colony prices it. `answered`'s second half is now "a row gives it, whichever colony's", so a raid the casting colony's row wins is no other colony's chase. `RoomVerdict.Rows` carries each caster's `RowAnswer`, which the colony's Brief reuses rather than pricing again. **The answerer** is ranked among the colonies that give the answer (the winning rows, else the kiting casters, else the colonies whose rangers are among the fewest that win): first by crossings (`World.hopsUnder`, built only where two colonies give it), then by bank, then by home name. **S4 as built** (on main 4f04f8c4). **The answerer casts; every colony the `Win` counts fights.** Every Brief carries `Answerer` and `Counted`. On a raided room's `Win`, `Answerer` is the world's answerer (`RoomVerdict.Answerer`) and `Counted` is every colony the verdict counts: whose row wins it (`Own`), or whose rangers are among the fewest that win it (`RoomVerdict.Bodies`, by name with their colony). Two gates read them. `Threats.answers` is who casts: the answerer alone, so `Quota.guardedAsks` sums a room's asks in the answerer's rows only. `Threats.pools` is who pools: the answerer and each counted colony, each sending its own bodies, so the Planner pools a raided room's Guard (guarded rooms, held exits, kills) only there. A colony neither answering nor counted neither pools nor casts. That is the S3 gate's goal, that neither colony sends half the answer, met without one colony moving another's bodies. The binding governs only a colony that crosses or neighbours the room: a colony whose own Guard the room is (`Facts.guardedRooms`: its declared outposts, the ranger and harassment rooms it casts, its children's homes) pools it and casts for it whoever answers, as before S4. Otherwise a neighbour's ranger winning a colony's outpost alone would leave the outpost unguarded under a `Win` that keeps its stand-down shut while its miners work on (a re-review finding). So a room cannot go unanswered: its own colony always pools it, the answerer is among the counted colonies, and each counted colony pools it, as a chase where its own row does not give it. Nothing binds a room no raider stands in, a `Kite` (the verdict names no body, and the ranger that kites the raid down is any colony's), a `Lose`, a home of ours (its raid is its own colony's Defend and its garrison's Guard, two Tasks, #552: the mother keeps her garrison's Guard though the child's rangers alone win it), or a Task of the colony's own; there every colony that claims it pools and casts, as before. The world map `Map<Task, Brief>` is the union of each colony's Briefs keyed by Task, each read through those two gates. ## Consequences

- **CPU.** This is not the reason for the epic. The saving is modest: about 1–3 ms of the 6–8 ms cut (the duplicate rooms' share plus the doubled scan derivation), and verdict pricing falls from up to 2 × views to 1 per raided room, which matters under a siege. The likely bigger win is Memory. The raid logs are 37% of it, and W17S29's transit rosters are parsed and stringified every tick. Any claim is an interleaved A/B with identical fingerprints.
- **The Memory migration runs on the reset tick** (S2, S5), already the most expensive tick (#442). A wrong merge reopens a stronghold room, so the merge has a dedicated test. A legacy per-colony leaf reads as absent once migrated.
- **The light tick (ADR 0082)** must read world danger. `glanceOf`'s forcing rule and `RoomRoles` are fed from the world layer. Otherwise a light tick misses a raid only the world layer saw: a shut room watched by vision alone.
- **The answerer is new behaviour.** It changes who walks where. `Recall` and the lend rule are re-derived from the lender's own world-side quiet test. "One colony pools each Task id" is an invariant with a test.
- **Fixtures move to one World plus N colonies.** Decide tests build a `ColonyView` today. That is a large but mechanical edit, and `ParallelSafetyTests` still holds: no fixture captures an Atlas. An arena capture then holds the whole picture, mother included (#532).
- **ADR 0085's Brief survives** with its Task keying. Only where it is built moves, from per colony to per world.
- **Ordering.** S1 touches `Sightings` and `Facts`, which #531 (shipped) touched too. The movement epic builds on the world layer and comes after it.

## Considered options

- **Keep patching each seam** (`ChildOutposts` keeps shut rooms, one more `Lendable`-style field per case). Rejected: every patch so far (#447, #448, #451, #484, #517, #518) smuggled one more world fact into a view, and #534 is a bug in one of them.
- **Share vision only (S1 alone).** It fixes #534, but leaves the two verdicts, the double latches and the transit rosters. Kept as the first stage, not as the end state.
- **One `decide` for all colonies over a merged view.** Rejected: ADR 0052's unit is the colony, because a colony's bank, stage and rows differ. Merging loses that, and every quota would be re-derived.
- **A world layer that also matches and casts centrally.** Rejected: the Matcher, quotas and spawns are per colony by ADR 0052 and work. The world layer stops at *who answers*, and the answering colony pools the Task.
